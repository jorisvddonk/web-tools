# Choose a diagram with the recommender

If you know what you want to show but not which format fits, the recommender narrows it down.

## Open it

Press **Find a diagram** in the toolbar of the [diagram renderer](https://jorisvddonk.github.io/web-tools/diagrams.html).

## Answer the questions

The first question asks what you are trying to show: a flow or architecture, messages over time, classes or data models, data or metrics, a business process, a mindmap, a schedule, or signals and ASCII art.

Each answer either asks a follow-up (for example *which kind of flow?*) or lands on a recommendation. The breadcrumb at the top shows the path you took, and **← Back** and **Start over** are always available.

## Read the result

The result names the format and a specific sample, explains why it fits, and renders a live preview using your current theme.

## Load it

Press **Load sample**. The modal closes, the format selector changes, the example is loaded into the editor and the diagram renders — ready to edit.

## Close without changing anything

Press the **×**, click outside the dialog or press <kbd>Esc</kbd>. Background scrolling is locked while the dialog is open, and focus returns to the button you opened it from.
